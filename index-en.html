Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our programs and admissions.

Campus Superia welcomes learners from international backgrounds wishing to develop their mastery of French, pursue an academic project in France, or strengthen their skills in a stimulating and demanding educational environment.
You can send your application directly via the website form or contact the Campus Superia team to start your registration.
Campus Superia was designed as a learning space where mastery of the French language becomes a lever for personal, academic, and professional growth. Each program is designed to accompany learners in building an ambitious and sustainable future.
Campus Superia offers French as a Foreign Language programs as well as vocational training allowing students to prepare for degree courses, notably towards BTS, and to access new academic or professional perspectives.
The programs allow students to strengthen language skills, pursue higher education in France, or develop qualifications valued in the job market.
