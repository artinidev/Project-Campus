Diagnostic Evaluation – CEFR Level Assessment (A1 to C1)

English Placement Test

Forming tomorrow's talents today. This test identifies your English level according to the CEFR framework.

Test Objective

This test aims to identify the candidate's proficiency level in English according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), from A1 to C1. It evaluates reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, and written production in order to guide the candidate toward the most suitable learning program.
